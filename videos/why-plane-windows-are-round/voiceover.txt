Square plane windows once killed people.

In 1952, the Comet became the first jet airliner.

Jets fly high, so the cabin is pumped full of air.

Every flight, the metal skin stretched and relaxed.

At the square corners, the stress was up to three times higher.

Two Comets broke apart in 1954. Cracks at the corners ripped the cabin open.

A round window has no corners, so the stress flows around it.

Follow for more things nobody explained.
