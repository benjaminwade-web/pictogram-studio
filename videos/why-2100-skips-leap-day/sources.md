# Sources

- Leap year rule: divisible by 4, except century years not divisible by 400. 1900 and 2100 are not leap years, 2000 was (US Naval Observatory): https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/leap_years
- A year (tropical year) is about 365.2422 days. The old 365.25-day average ran about 0.0078 days long, which is about 11 minutes a year (US Naval Observatory): https://aa.usno.navy.mil/faq/leap_years
- Ten days were dropped in October 1582: 15 October followed 4 October (Claus Tondering, Calendar FAQ): https://www.tondering.dk/claus/cal/gregorian.php

## Notes

"365 and a quarter" is the rounded figure the old calendar assumed. The 11 minutes is my conversion of 0.0078 days. The reform was ordered by Pope Gregory XIII.
