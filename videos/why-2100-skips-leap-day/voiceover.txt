The year 2100 will skip leap day.

A year is not 365 days. Earth needs about 365 and a quarter.

So every four years we add one extra day.

But that is slightly too much. About 11 minutes too much, every year.

By 1582 it had grown to ten days. So the Pope deleted ten days.

His new rule: century years skip it, unless they divide by 400.

1900 skipped. 2000 kept it. 2100 skips again.
