# Buffett dug for fifty years
Idea: Almost all of Warren Buffett's money arrived after he turned 65
Style: Neon terminal. Shape: 9:16. Length: 32.8 seconds, 8 scenes, 74 spoken words.

## Retention check
12 of 12 pass.

## Files
- `check-sheet.jpg`: one picture per scene.
- `voiceover.txt`: the script to paste into ElevenLabs, one paragraph per scene.
- `post.txt`: caption, hashtags and other hooks to test.
- `sources.md`: where each fact came from.
- `buffett-dug-for-fifty-years.json`: the project. Paste its text into "Load a project" in Pictogram Studio to edit it.

## Scenes

## To add the voice

1. Paste `voiceover.txt` into ElevenLabs and download the MP3. If you want it faster, set the speed in ElevenLabs, not in CapCut, so the pictures stay in step.
2. Send the MP3 back and ask for the video to be fitted to it, or add it yourself in Pictogram Studio (Voiceover box, then "Fit scenes to the voice", then "Record video").
